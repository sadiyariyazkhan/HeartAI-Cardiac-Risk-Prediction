About HeartAI

HeartAI is a specialized clinical decision-support engine designed to bridge advanced machine learning with everyday cardiovascular diagnostics. By analyzing patient vitals, laboratory biomarkers, and resting ECG indicators simultaneously, our platform delivers accurate, real-time risk stratification.

🎯

Our Mission

Empowering healthcare providers with actionable predictive intelligence to enable proactive early intervention and minimize misdiagnoses.

Machine Learning Engine
